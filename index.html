About

From decommissioning to informed reuse

Photovoltaic deployment is expanding rapidly, but many modules leave service before the end of their technical lifetime. CIRCULARePV investigates how operational, environmental and diagnostic data can be combined to identify modules with real second-life potential.

The project links field information with laboratory characterization, probabilistic classification and application-specific decision rules, aiming to make PV reuse more transparent, evidence-based and fit for purpose.

Research

Three connected research pillars

From field data to actionable second-life decisions.

01

Predictive markers for reuse

Harmonise operational, environmental and O&M information with laboratory diagnostics such as visual inspection, I-V testing, electroluminescence and insulation tests.

02

Probabilistic classification

Build data-driven health assessment models that classify module condition while accounting for uncertainty and providing interpretable indicators.

03

Fit-for-purpose deployment

Translate module condition into application-specific reuse decisions for contexts such as agrivoltaics, building integration and other second-life applications.
